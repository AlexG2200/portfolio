// About Alex

Building SkillThrough Real Projects

I'm a Computer Science student at Middle Tennessee State University focused on turning ideas, coursework, and practical problems into working software.

My interest in software comes from the same place most of my best projects start: seeing a problem and wanting to build something that makes it clearer, faster, or easier to manage. I like projects that connect code to real workflows, whether that means automating data, building a useful command-line tool, or turning a rough idea into a clean web experience.

At MTSU, I'm building my foundation in computer science while using personal projects to make those concepts practical. C++ has helped me think more carefully about logic, structure, and fundamentals. Python has become my go-to language for automation, data handling, and tools that remove repetitive work. Web development gives me a way to present those ideas clearly and make them usable for other people.

I am still early in my career, but I care about building like someone who wants the work to last. That means documenting projects, improving structure, learning from mistakes, and making each build more useful than the last one.

Right now, I'm focused on strengthening my portfolio for internships, freelance opportunities, and software projects where practical problem solving matters. My goal is to keep building a body of work that shows progress, discipline, and the ability to turn ideas into something real.
