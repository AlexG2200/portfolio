// About Alex

Building SkillThrough Real Projects

I'm a Computer Science student at Middle Tennessee State University focused on turning ideas, coursework, and practical problems into working software.

I have always been curious about how technology works. As a kid, I wanted to understand why something was broken, how a device worked, or what made a program behave the way it did. That curiosity eventually made me my family’s personal “tech guy.” Whenever someone had a technology-related problem, I was the person they came to.

Being the family “tech guy” taught me how to troubleshoot, research solutions, and explain technical problems clearly in Spanish to my family. Over time, I began to see technology as something I could understand and use to build software of my own.

Now, I’m studying Computer Science at MTSU and working toward becoming a software developer. I challenge myself with every project I build, focusing on practical tools, clean structure, and steady improvement. My goal is to create useful software that benefits the people who use it while continuing to follow the curiosity that first drew me to technology.
